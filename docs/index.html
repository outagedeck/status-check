What this proves

A healthy result means OutageDeck's public endpoint and its backing data path answered this browser. It does not mean every provider is healthy, or that an official provider feed is complete.

Why this exists

A Grafana practitioner pointed out that an availability product cannot ask people to trust a status page served by the same deployment. They were right. This is the first check moved outside that boundary.
